# SecureVote — Risk Register (hypothetical business)

> Educational project for the course Introduction to Finance. Not a real product, service or investment offer. All probabilities, impacts and costs are **student assumptions**. Mitigation costs marked "in model" are already included in [`FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`](FINANCIAL_MODEL.md); others are additional and **not** in the base case (they would worsen the NPV).

**Scales:** Likelihood (L) and Impact (I) from 1 (very low) to 5 (very high). **Rating = L × I**: 1–6 low · 8–12 medium · 15–25 high.

## Highest-priority risks

1. **R1 (20)** — legal validity of electronic voting for joint-stock companies in Kazakhstan.
2. **M1 (16)** and **P1 (16)** — adoption and the transparent-ballot limitation.
3. **F1 (15)** — funding gap in pessimistic scenarios.

The sum of additional mitigation costs that are **not** in the base case (≈ 14.5 m KZT one-off: pilots 3.0 + hardware wallets/multisig 1.0 + pen test 4.0 + secret-ballot research 6.0 + IP agreements 0.5; plus 2.7 m KZT/year: node redundancy 1.5 + DDoS protection 1.2) would lower the base-case NPV further; this is deliberately reported rather than hidden.
